01 / About

Engineering from start to finish

MECar is a student racing team affiliated with the College of Engineering at Yonsei University.

Our members turn ideas into working vehicles through design, analysis, sourcing, manufacturing, assembly, and testing. We bring what we learn on track into the next design.

Since 2026, our Baja program has been joined by a Formula team aiming for a Formula Student Korea debut in 2027.

02 / Vehicles

Two vehicles, one team

Baja

Electric off-road

Built to endure

Our Baja team keeps improving its electric off-road vehicle using experience from testing and competition. Durability and stable handling guide each iteration.

Formula

2027 goal

Built from the ground up

Established in 2026, our Formula team is developing MECar's first Formula car, aiming to compete at Formula Student Korea in 2027.
